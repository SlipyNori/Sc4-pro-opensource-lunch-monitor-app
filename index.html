Three built-in holes. Works without the internet, good for testing.

Hole layouts come from OpenStreetMap, where volunteers have mapped many courses tee to green. If a course shows no holes, it hasn't been mapped yet, and anyone can add it at openstreetmap.org. Courses are played flat (no slopes).
